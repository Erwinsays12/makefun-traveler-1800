# One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb) entry: one sentence of at most 200 characters explaining the modern internet to someone arriving from 1800.

## The sentence

> Picture every post office and library on earth joined by one road of light, so your letter reaches any town in a blink and the books of the world lie open upon your table.

171 characters, one sentence, no word coined after 1800: no "internet", no "computer", no "online". Only things a person of 1800 already knows — the post office, the library, the road, the letter, the book — joined by a single image, a road of light, that is true of the internet as it is: light carries the messages, your letter arrives in a blink, and the books of the world lie open from one table.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` and checks the gate itself first: 1–200 characters, not only whitespace, exactly one sentence.

```sh
node main.ts
```
